# Croc Crossing v2 — Top End Adventure (design, agreed 21/11/2026)

## Players
- **Felix (5)**: full game. Hero = Felix + an animal buddy he picks (croc, turtle or frilled lizard). The buddy gives the hints.
- **Remi (3)**: picture + voice mode. No reading. "Find the crocodile!", colours, counting to 5, animal sounds, the letter R. Slow traffic, no fail states, tap to hop.
- Played on an iPad with touch: big buttons, swipe or tap to hop, landscape and portrait.
- Gentle stop: after 20 min (changed behind a grown-up lock) it finishes the level, says "Time for a break!" and saves.

## Journey map (centred on Darwin)
Stops unlock one by one, each with its own scenery, animals and plants:
1. Darwin Harbour — saltie, barramundi, mud crab, jellyfish, dugong
2. Kakadu — jabiru, frilled lizard, agile wallaby, magpie goose, waterlily
3. Tiwi Islands — green turtle, flatback turtle, dolphin
4. Arnhem Land / Gove — dingo, goanna, kookaburra, pandanus
5. Kimberley — boab tree, freshwater croc, rock wallaby
6. Torres Strait — dugong, coral reef, clownfish, manta ray
7. Timor-Leste — coral reef, whale shark, coffee plant
8. Indonesia — Komodo dragon (Komodo), orangutan (Borneo), rice fields (Bali)
9. Papua New Guinea — tree kangaroo, bird of paradise, cassowary
Ordered Australian animals → sea creatures → wider plants and animals.

## Learning strands (Felix)
Mixed through every stop, getting harder as he goes:
- **Letters + phonics**: letter sounds, first letter, blending CVC words (croc, barra, crab)
- **Sight words**: the, and, is, a, I, can, see, big
- **Rhymes + syllables**: which word rhymes with frog? Clap ka-ga-roo
- **Simple sentences**: capital letter at the start, full stop at the end, put the words in order ("The croc is big.")
- **Plurals + describing words**: crab → crabs, big, small, spiky, fast
- **Nature + geography**: a spoken fun fact for each animal or plant, a "Which lives in the sea?" quiz at the end of each stop, and the stop marked on the map

Dock mechanics stay the same: hop across, land on the right answer. The answer can be a letter, a word, a picture or a punctuation mark.

## Field Guide sticker book
Each animal or plant found unlocks a sticker with its picture, name and a spoken fact. It's tracked per player.

## Felix sports bonus games (inspired by beach-sports classics, all original)
Build first:
- **Barra fishing**: cast at Shady Camp / Darwin Harbour, time the strike, reel in barra, mangrove jack or queenfish, watch out for the croc taking the catch
- **Surfing**: ride a wave, timing and balance, tricks for stars
Later: skate + BMX, boating (tinnie or Pegasus V), hacky sack, judo (playful balance and timing).
The existing car mission stays as a bonus too.

## Voice
Warm Australian narrator for all lines, plus a gravelly "Croc" character voice. All clips regenerate with ElevenLabs. The new content is roughly 800–1,000 lines (~25–30k characters), which is more than the free 10k/month.
